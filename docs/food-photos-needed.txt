FOOD PHOTOS FOR THE HAZRATGUNJ MENU

How it works
1. Put the photos in one folder. Name each file exactly as in the list below
   (the part before " — "), e.g.  paneer-tikka.jpg
   .jpg, .jpeg, .png and .webp all work. Capitals and spaces do not matter, and the
   printed dish name also works when it is unique ("Paneer Tikka.jpg").
2. Send me the folder path. I run:  npm run build:photos -- "<folder>"
   Each photo is cropped to 4:5, given the same bright grade and put on its dish.
   Any file I cannot match to a dish is listed back to you.

How to shoot: portrait (upright) or square, daylight or bright warm light, the dish
filling most of the frame, plain table or wooden board behind it, no hands, no menu
cards, no phones, no people.

The three bottled drinks sold at MRP (soft drinks, diet drinks, mineral water) need no photo.
